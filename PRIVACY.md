# Privacy Policy — KPI Card by Posy

_Last updated: July 24, 2026_

**KPI Card by Posy** is a Power BI custom visual published by Posy. Posy is a brand of **Platform Apps LLC**.

## What data the visual processes

The visual renders the fields you bind to it (Value, Trend axis, Comparison, Target, Conditional formatting basis, and Tooltips). All processing happens locally, inside the Power BI visual sandbox, on the device where the report is viewed.

## What the visual does NOT do

- It makes **no network requests** of any kind. No report data, user data, or telemetry ever leaves Power BI.
- It does **not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information.
- It does **not use cookies** or any form of tracking.
- It does **not access** external services, resources, or storage.

The visual is submitted for Microsoft's **Power BI certification**, whose review independently verifies that the visual does not access external services or resources.

## Data handled by Power BI

Your report data is governed by Microsoft Power BI and your organization's policies. This visual adds no data handling beyond rendering inside the report.
